How do you want to play?

  • Each case has 5 clues. Clue 1 is shown; reveal more when you need them.
  • Answer after clue 1 = 5 points, after clue 2 = 4 … after clue 5 = 1.
  • A wrong answer costs 1 point and opens the next clue.
  • Steal: if the team on turn answers wrong, the next team may steal at the current points. If the steal fails, play goes back to the team on turn.
  • Every case ends with a teaching card: the mechanism, what to look for, and the physiotherapy focus.
